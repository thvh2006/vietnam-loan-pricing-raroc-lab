SHADOW USE
01

Decision boundary

Eligibility and affordability gates precede price. A floor above the management cap routes to manual review.

02

Evidence boundary

Applications are synthetic. PD, LGD and operating costs are assumptions, not estimates calibrated to a lender book.

03

Validation

Python and Excel reconcile; monotonicity, policy boundaries and deterministic outputs are automatically tested.

04

Production gap

Outcome calibration, prepayment, delinquency states, collections, fairness, overrides and realised-margin backtesting remain required.
